About the Institute

Meerut Institute of Engineering & Technology (MIET) is a premier educational institution dedicated to advancing scientific research and practical knowledge in biotechnology and microbiology.

Our Hands-on Training of Biological Tools & Techniques (HTBTT) program provides students with industry-oriented practical exposure to modern biotechnology techniques, laboratory equipment, and research methodologies.

This program bridges the gap between academic learning and professional practice, preparing students for careers in biotechnology, pharmaceutical, food, and healthcare industries.
