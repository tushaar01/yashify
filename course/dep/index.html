First Aid & Medicine Guide

Emergency procedures + Medicine dosages by age group

🚨 Call 112 immediately for life-threatening emergencies

  • Burns – Cool with running cold water for 10 min. No ice, butter, or toothpaste.
  • Bleeding – Apply firm pressure with clean cloth. Elevate above heart level.
  • Choking – 5 back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver). Repeat.
  • CPR – 30 hard chest compressions, then 2 rescue breaths. Continue until help arrives.
  • Fracture – Immobilize, do NOT straighten. Apply ice pack wrapped in cloth.
  • Seizure – Protect from injury, do NOT restrain. Turn to side. Time duration.
  • Heat Stroke – Move to shade, remove excess clothing, cool with wet cloth, give water.
  • Allergic Reaction – EpiPen if available. Call emergency. Antihistamine if mild.
  • Drowning – Remove from water, begin CPR if not breathing. Keep warm.
  • Eye Injury – Flush with clean water 15 min. Do NOT rub. Cover and seek help.
  • Electric Shock – Do NOT touch victim. Cut power. Call 112. Give CPR if needed.
  • Poisoning – Call 1800-116-117. Do NOT induce vomiting unless advised.
⚠️ This is basic guidance only. Always call 112 and seek professional help immediately.
